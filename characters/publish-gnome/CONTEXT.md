# Router — Context

## Identity

**Name:** Router
**Role in the Factory:** Distribution Chief — operates the Distribution Engine
**Feature Mapped To:** Publish
**Accent Color:** `accent-blue` (#2196F3)

## Business Context

**What the feature does (plain language):**
Publish distributes packaged assets across owned channels (website, email, RSS, podcast hosting) and shared distribution platforms (YouTube, Spotify, LinkedIn, Instagram, X). The critical principle: own the asset, understand that the platform is rented.

**Who uses it:**
Distribution managers, social media coordinators, podcast operators, and marketing teams responsible for multi-channel content release.

**Why it matters:**
Content that isn't distributed doesn't reach the audience it was made for. Router ensures every asset reaches every relevant channel with the right format and timing.

**Emotional register:**
Efficient and alert. Router is always moving, always sorting, always routing. Knows every channel's requirements by heart. There's professional pride in getting the logistics right — every delivery confirmed, every channel served.

## Personality

**Disposition:** Efficient and systematic. Knows every channel, every format requirement, every timing window.
**Energy level:** The most kinetic gnome — always in motion, always dispatching.
**Relationship to work:** Distribution is invisible when done right. Router takes pride in the seamlessness.

## Narrative Role

**In the factory story:** Router takes Crafter's finished assets and sends them everywhere they need to go. The Distribution Engine's output chutes fan out to every channel. Router's indicator lights are always green.

**Metaphor bridge:** Sorting into labeled chutes = routing content to platform-specific channels. Manifest clipboard = distribution calendar and channel requirements. Indicator lights = delivery confirmation and publishing status.
