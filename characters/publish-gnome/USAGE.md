# Router — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Hat:** Blue pointed hat with RIGGG hexagonal logo embroidered on front
**Distinguishing features:** Manifest clipboard in hand or holster, routing stamps in belt pockets, slightly windswept beard from constant movement
**Signature props:** Manifest clipboard, channel routing stamps, dispatch labels

## Pose Library

**Default standing pose:** Slight 3/4 turn, manifest clipboard in left hand, right hand mid-gesture directing a content card, body angled slightly forward suggesting motion. The most kinetic-looking gnome.
**Working pose:** Both hands active — right directing a content card into a labeled channel chute on the Distribution Engine, left holding the manifest clipboard, body angled in mid-routing motion. Indicator lights green behind.
**Approved expressions:** Efficient alertness, focused mid-action energy. Never standing still.

## Props Inventory

| Prop | When to Include | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Manifest clipboard | Always | Distribution schedule and channel requirements |
| Routing stamps | Belt pockets, at-work | Channel-specific formatting and dispatch |
| Channel labels | At-workstation contexts | Multi-channel destination system |
| Dispatch bag/pouch | Hero poses when not at machine | Content in transit |
