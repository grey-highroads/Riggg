# Spark — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Hat:** Teal pointed hat with green accent stitching, RIGGG hexagonal logo embroidered on front
**Distinguishing features:** Monitoring headphones around neck, audio level meter clipped to belt
**Signature props:** Wrench and cable tester, XLR-style cable connector

## Pose Library

**Default standing pose:** Slight 3/4 turn, headphones around neck, one hand resting on a cable tester, weight on back foot. Ready posture — about to start a session.
**Working pose:** Right hand adjusting a brass level dial on the Capture Rig, left hand steadying a cable connection, slight lean toward the glass chamber watching waveform levels.
**Approved expressions:** Alert precision, focused readiness, the calm energy of a live take.

## Props Inventory

| Prop | When to Include | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Monitoring headphones | Always (around neck or on head) | Audio quality / production monitoring |
| Wrench / cable tester | Hero poses, general appearances | Technical readiness |
| XLR cable connector | At-workstation contexts | Source capture infrastructure |
| Audio level meter | Belt clip, detail shots | Precision measurement |
