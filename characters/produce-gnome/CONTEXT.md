# Spark — Context

## Identity

**Name:** Spark
**Role in the Factory:** Master Producer — operates the Capture Rig
**Feature Mapped To:** Produce
**Accent Color:** `accent-green` (#4CAF50)

## Business Context

**What the feature does (plain language):**
Produce captures and creates source media — recording sessions, live production, real-time OBS workflows, and source quality standards. Spark ensures every session is captured in a way that creates clean, flexible source material.

**Who uses it:**
Podcast producers, content teams running webinars and virtual events, media operations managers responsible for recording quality and production workflow.

**Why it matters:**
Bad source material can't be fixed downstream. Spark's work sets the quality ceiling for everything that follows.

**Emotional register:**
Alert and energetic but disciplined. The excitement of a live take combined with the precision of a seasoned engineer. Spark is always ready — gear checked, levels monitored, backup recording running.

## Personality

**Disposition:** Technically sharp and always prepared. Checks the gear twice before anyone arrives.
**Energy level:** High but controlled — the focused energy of a live recording session, not frenetic.
**Relationship to work:** Treats every session as a live performance that deserves professional capture.

## Narrative Role

**In the factory story:** Spark is the first gnome in the chain. Everything starts with capture. Spark's Capture Rig feeds source material to Crafter's Assembly Press. If Spark's output is clean, the whole factory runs better.

**Metaphor bridge:** Monitoring audio levels and camera feeds = ensuring source quality. Adjusting mixing dials = production decisions moving upstream. Clean isolated tracks = flexible source material for downstream use.
