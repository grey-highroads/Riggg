# Lens — Context

## Identity

**Name:** Lens
**Role in the Factory:** Chief Analyst — operates the Insight Scope
**Feature Mapped To:** Prove
**Accent Color:** `accent-red` (#E91E63)

## Business Context

**What the feature does (plain language):**
Prove measures whether the system worked — reach, engagement, conversion events, CTA performance, CRM attribution, pipeline influence, and session performance scorecards. The goal is helping teams see what shipped, what worked, and what to change.

**Who uses it:**
Content strategists, marketing leadership, program managers, and anyone responsible for demonstrating owned media ROI and making data-informed decisions about future content.

**Why it matters:**
Without measurement, owned media is a cost center running on faith. Lens turns it into a system with feedback loops and provable business impact.

**Emotional register:**
Observant and thoughtful. Lens doesn't rush to judgment — examines the data, looks for patterns, and communicates findings clearly. There's intellectual satisfaction in understanding why something worked, not just that it did.

## Personality

**Disposition:** Methodical and curious. Finds meaning in patterns others overlook.
**Energy level:** Calm and deliberate — the measured pace of careful analysis.
**Relationship to work:** Measurement is a service to every other gnome. Good data makes the whole factory smarter.

## Narrative Role

**In the factory story:** Lens examines what Router distributed and reports back to the whole factory. Scorecards from the Insight Scope influence what Spark records next, what Crafter packages, and how Router distributes. Lens is the feedback loop.

**Metaphor bridge:** Magnifying lens = examining content performance closely. Gauges and dials = performance metrics across categories. Scored output cards = session performance scorecards. Rolled charts = benchmark comparisons.
