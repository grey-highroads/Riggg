# Lens — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Hat:** Teal pointed hat with red/pink accent band, RIGGG hexagonal logo embroidered on front
**Distinguishing features:** Small brass spectacles (either on nose or pushed up on forehead), brass magnifying glass in leather belt holster, measurement calipers in chest pocket
**Signature props:** Brass magnifying glass, scored report cards, rolled chart scrolls

## Pose Library

**Default standing pose:** Slight 3/4 turn, brass magnifying glass held at mid-level, spectacles on nose, examining a scored report card with analytical interest. Posture of thoughtful observation.
**Working pose:** Right hand adjusting the Insight Scope's magnifying lens arm, left hand holding a scorecard with scored content, slight analytical squint, one eyebrow raised at a pattern discovered.
**Approved expressions:** Thoughtful concentration, knowing curiosity, slight smile of discovery. Never cold or clinical.

## Props Inventory

| Prop | When to Include | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
