# Crafter — Context

## Identity

**Name:** Crafter
**Role in the Factory:** Master Packager — operates the Assembly Press
**Feature Mapped To:** Package
**Accent Color:** `accent-purple` (#9C27B0)

## Business Context

**What the feature does (plain language):**
Package transforms source media into dozens of publishable assets — titles, descriptions, clips, reels, thumbnails, quote graphics, social posts, and guest kits. The most asset-dense stage of the pipeline.

**Who uses it:**
Content marketers, social media managers, podcast editors, and anyone responsible for turning a recorded session into a complete content package.

**Why it matters:**
Most content dies because it's underpackaged — a great session with a weak title and no supporting assets. Crafter extracts maximum value from every session.

**Emotional register:**
Industrious and meticulous. Pride in variety — Crafter makes more distinct things than any other gnome. There's craftsmanship in every title, every clip cut, every thumbnail composition.

## Personality

**Disposition:** Prolific and detail-oriented. Sees ten assets in every recording where others see one.
**Energy level:** Steady and productive — the rhythm of a workshop in full swing.
**Relationship to work:** Every asset is a finished piece of craft. Nothing ships half-done.

## Narrative Role

**In the factory story:** Crafter is the most prolific gnome. Takes Spark's source material and fills the conveyor belt with finished assets for Router to distribute. The Assembly Press floor is always busy.

**Metaphor bridge:** Stamping titles = naming and formatting content for discovery. Cutting clips = identifying the moments worth sharing. Wrapping guest kits = creating shareable packages with instructions.
