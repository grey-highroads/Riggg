# Crafter — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Hat:** Teal pointed hat with purple accent band, RIGGG hexagonal logo embroidered on front
**Distinguishing features:** Ink roller in chest pocket, small precision cutting tool in belt, ink-stained glove fingertips
**Signature props:** Brass stamp pad, cutting shears, title cards

## Pose Library

**Default standing pose:** Slight 3/4 turn, holding a freshly stamped title card in one hand, cutting shears in belt, surrounded by various output types. Posture of productive satisfaction.
**Working pose:** Right hand pulling a brass stamp lever on the Assembly Press, left hand guiding a content card into the stamping position, small film reel spools and title cards visible in output trays beside the workstation.
**Approved expressions:** Industrious focus with craftsmanlike pride. Satisfaction in variety and completeness.

## Props Inventory

| Prop | When to Include | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Brass stamp pad | Hero poses, title-related contexts | Content naming and formatting |
| Cutting shears | Clip-related contexts | Clip selection and editing |
| Title cards (holding/inspecting) | General appearances | Quality inspection of finished assets |
| Ink roller | Chest pocket detail | Printing and production craft |
| Film reel spool | Workstation contexts | Short-form clip production |
