# Keeper — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Hat:** Teal pointed hat with amber accent band, RIGGG hexagonal logo embroidered on front
**Distinguishing features:** Reading spectacles (often pushed up onto forehead), leather-bound ledger in belt holster, brass key ring on belt, gloves worn soft from years of careful handling
**Signature props:** Leather-bound ledger, filing keys, catalog cards

## Pose Library

**Default standing pose:** Slight 3/4 turn, leather-bound ledger held open in left hand, right hand resting on a brass key ring, spectacles pushed up on forehead, calm protective posture. The most grounded and settled-looking gnome.
**Working pose:** Right hand carefully sliding a content card into a brass drawer slot in the Memory Vault's card catalog, left hand resting on the open leather ledger for reference, spectacles on nose, gentle deliberate movement.
**Approved expressions:** Warm protective pride, gentle stewardship, the quiet knowing of someone who has everything organized. The oldest-feeling gnome in demeanor.

## Props Inventory

| Prop | When to Include | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Leather-bound ledger | Always (in hand or holster) | Metadata, topic maps, institutional memory |
| Brass key ring | Belt, hero poses | Access and organization authority |
| Catalog cards | At-work, filing contexts | Content indexing and tagging |
| Reading spectacles | Always (on nose or forehead) | Careful reading, attention to detail |
| Small brass lamp | Workstation contexts | The amber warmth of the archive |
