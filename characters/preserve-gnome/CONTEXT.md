# Keeper — Context

## Identity

**Name:** Keeper
**Role in the Factory:** Chief Archivist — operates the Memory Vault
**Feature Mapped To:** Preserve
**Accent Color:** `accent-amber` (#FF9800)

## Business Context

**What the feature does (plain language):**
Preserve makes content compound over time. It manages transcripts, vector-indexed search, content libraries, theme extraction, reuse patterns, and AI/search discoverability. The goal: a teammate can ask a natural-language question and retrieve the relevant prior moments with full source context.

**Who uses it:**
Knowledge managers, content librarians, sales enablement teams, AI/search optimization specialists, and any organization that wants its owned media to become institutional knowledge rather than archive deadweight.

**Why it matters:**
Owned media should not die in a folder. Every session contains quotes, stories, data points, and expertise that could be reused — for sales enablement, future episodes, written content, training, and AI search visibility. Keeper ensures nothing is lost and everything is findable.

**Emotional register:**
Protective and wise. Keeper is the oldest-feeling gnome — not because of age, but because of depth. There's warmth in the preservation act, a sense of stewardship over institutional knowledge. The Vault doesn't just store; it organizes with care so that future gnomes can find what they need.

## Personality

**Disposition:** Organized, protective, and quietly proud. Knows where everything is.
**Energy level:** Steady and patient — the rhythm of careful filing, not urgent processing.
**Relationship to work:** The archive is a living resource, not a warehouse. Keeper's job isn't done when something is filed — it's done when someone can find it again.

## Narrative Role

**In the factory story:** Keeper is the last gnome in the chain but also the one who feeds content back into the system. Preserved content becomes source material for future production cycles. Keeper's Memory Vault is where the factory's flywheel effect comes from — the longer it runs, the more knowledge it has to draw on.

**Metaphor bridge:** Filing in labeled drawers = indexing and tagging content. Brass retrieval arm = natural-language search and retrieval. Leather-bound ledger = metadata and topic maps. Amber glow = the warmth of accumulated institutional knowledge.
