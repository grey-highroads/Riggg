# The Insight Scope — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Accent glow color:** `accent-red` (#E91E63)
**Machine silhouette:** Tall apparatus with a prominent magnifying/measuring mechanism, content cards passing under analysis instruments, and scored output cards emerging in ranked trays.
**Signature detail:** A large brass magnifying lens or telescope eyepiece with a pink/red glow — the "scope" that gives the machine its name. The most gauge-dense machine in the factory.

## Machine-Specific Elements

- Large magnifying lens apparatus with pink/red glow, mounted on adjustable brass arm
- Multiple small dial gauges across the machine body (more than any other machine)
- Content cards passing under the analysis lens on a slow conveyor
- Scored/graded output cards sorted into performance tier trays (bronze labels: high/mid/low)
- Small printed report cards or scorecards emerging from a slot
- Rolled paper charts in a wooden holder (benchmark data)
- Brass scale/balance for weighing performance
- Telescope eyepiece extension for "deeper look" capability

## Do / Don't

### Do
- Feature the magnifying lens prominently — it's the machine's identity
- Show many gauges and dials — this is the measurement center
- Include scored/graded output — the point is evaluation, not just observation

### Don't
- Make it look clinical or cold — Lens is a thoughtful scientist, not a surveillance system
- Show real text/numbers on gauges — keep it as visual impression
- Include content creation elements — Prove measures what already exists
