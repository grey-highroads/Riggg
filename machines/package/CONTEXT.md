# The Assembly Press — Context

## Feature Identity

**Machine name:** The Assembly Press
**RIGGG feature:** Package
**Accent color:** `accent-purple` (#9C27B0)
**Assigned gnome:** Crafter

## Business Context

**What this feature does (plain language):**
Package turns source media into useful assets. A recorded session is raw material — Package is where it becomes titles, descriptions, show notes, clips, reels, thumbnails, quote graphics, chapters, and guest-share kits. This is the most asset-dense stage of the pipeline, producing more distinct deliverables than any other.

**The problem it solves:**
Teams record great conversations and then underpackage them. They publish the full episode with a weak title and no supporting assets. The content dies because nobody surfaced the moments worth sharing, wrote the copy worth reading, or created the visuals worth clicking. The session had value; the packaging didn't extract it.

**The outcome it delivers:**
A complete asset package per session: ranked title options, structured descriptions for RSS and YouTube, timestamped chapters, 3-5 short-form clips, vertical reels, thumbnails with hook patterns, quote graphics, social post copy for multiple platforms and post types, and guest-share kits with clips, captions, and instructions.

**Where it sits in the workflow:**
Second in the chain. Produce → **Package** → Publish → Prove → Preserve. Takes raw source material and transforms it into publishable, shareable, platform-ready assets.

**Quality question (from the framework):**
"Can someone understand, click, watch, listen, share, or reuse this asset without extra explanation?"

## What Gets Made Here (Owned Assets)

**Long-form:** Mastered video/audio episodes, clean transcripts, show notes, chapter markers
**Titles & descriptions:** Episode titles (10 pattern styles), RSS descriptions (10 pattern styles), YouTube descriptions, SEO page copy
**Short-form video:** Clips (horizontal), reels (vertical — 5 pattern styles), audiograms
**Graphics:** Thumbnails (5 pattern styles), quote graphics (5 pattern styles), stat callouts, audiogram cards
**Social copy:** Episode announcements, guest tags, quote shares, thread breakdowns, key takeaways, clip companions, evergreen reshares — each with platform-specific variants for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Instagram
**Guest assets:** Guest-share packages (clip + caption + instructions)

## Emotional Register

**How this feature should feel:**
Craftsmanlike and prolific. This is the most industrious part of the factory — the workshop where raw material becomes a dozen different finished goods. There's pride in the variety and completeness of what comes out. The Assembly Press doesn't rush or cut corners; it extracts maximum value from every session.

Productive and meticulous. "One session. Dozens of assets. Every one crafted to stand on its own."

## Metaphor Mapping

**Physical metaphor:** A multi-stage assembly press — raw material enters, passes through stamping, cutting, folding, and finishing stations, and emerges as a variety of distinct finished products. The machine has the most visible output variety of any in the factory: cards, reels on small spools, framed graphics, bundled guest kits.

**Input → Output:**
- **What goes in:** Source files from the Capture Rig (raw recordings, isolated tracks)
- **What the machine does:** Titles, clips, formats, brands, sizes, and packages
- **What comes out:** Complete asset packages — clips on spools, titled cards, thumbnail prints, guest kits in wrapped bundles

**Why this metaphor works:**
Packaging is literally assembly — taking one thing and making many things from it. The press metaphor captures both the precision (stamping, cutting) and the volume (multiple output types from a single input).
