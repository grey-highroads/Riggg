# The Capture Rig — Context

## Feature Identity

**Machine name:** The Capture Rig
**RIGGG feature:** Produce
**Accent color:** `accent-green` (#4CAF50)
**Assigned gnome:** Spark

## Business Context

**What this feature does (plain language):**
Produce is where owned media begins. It covers the entire capture and production layer — recording sessions, live production, real-time production with OBS or equivalent tools, hardware standards, and source quality specifications. The goal is to create clean, flexible source material that the rest of the system can work with.

**The problem it solves:**
Most teams treat recording as an afterthought. They use whatever platform is convenient, accept whatever quality comes out, and hand downstream teams degraded source material. Bad source audio and video can't be fixed in post. Poor capture decisions compound through every subsequent stage — packaging, publishing, measurement, and preservation all suffer when the raw material is weak.

**The outcome it delivers:**
Sessions captured to a production standard that already feels like a produced show before final mastering begins. Isolated tracks per speaker, branded overlays, lower thirds, scene plans, and backup recordings. Delivery timelines compress from traditional 7-10 day post-production to 3-5 day packages because production decisions moved upstream.

**Where it sits in the workflow:**
First in the chain. Produce → Package → Publish → Prove → Preserve. Everything downstream depends on source quality. This is the foundation.

**Quality question (from the framework):**
"Was the session captured in a way that creates clean, flexible source material?"

## What Gets Made Here (Owned Assets)

- Video master files
- Audio master files
- Isolated tracks (per speaker)
- Raw footage and B-roll
- Session transcripts (raw)
- Scene plans and branded overlays
- Lower thirds and intro/outro sequences

## Key Patterns (from the Index)

- OBS Real-Time Production Standard (Riggg Score: 5)
- Recording standards: remote isolated, hybrid, in-studio multi-camera, Zoom local/cloud, StreamYard, Google Meet
- Audio and video mastering standards
- Export specs

## Emotional Register

**How this feature should feel:**
Energetic and precise. This is where the work begins — the moment of capture. There's excitement in creation but discipline in execution. The Capture Rig isn't sloppy or improvisational; it's a well-prepared studio that's ready before the guest arrives.

**Tone for copy about this feature:**
Confident and technical. "Your source material sets the ceiling for everything that follows. Produce raises that ceiling."

## Metaphor Mapping

**Physical metaphor:** A precision recording apparatus — microphones, cameras, mixing boards, and monitoring equipment, all channeled through a central glass chamber where raw audio/video energy is captured and concentrated. The machine has prominent input connections (cables, feeds coming in) and outputs clean, glowing source material.

**Input → Output:**
- **What goes in:** Live conversation, raw expertise, unstructured knowledge
- **What the machine does:** Captures, isolates, monitors, overlays, and produces in real-time
- **What comes out:** Production-quality source files — clean, separated, branded, ready for packaging

**Why this metaphor works:**
Recording is literally capture — taking something ephemeral (a conversation) and making it tangible (a file). The Capture Rig visualizes this as energy being channeled and contained, which maps naturally to the glass-chamber-with-glow visual language.
