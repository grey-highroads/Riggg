# The Capture Rig — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Accent glow color:** `accent-green` (#4CAF50)
**Machine silhouette:** Upright apparatus with prominent microphone/camera inputs on top, a central glass capture chamber with green glow, mixing controls in the middle, and output trays with source file cards at the base.
**Signature detail:** A large studio microphone arm extending from the top of the machine, with a visible waveform pattern inside the glass chamber — representing live audio/video being captured.

## Composition Rules

**Standard layout:**
```
[Microphone arm / camera input] — [Glass capture chamber with green glow] — [Mixing controls]
                                           ↑
                                    [Output: source file cards on conveyor]
                                           ↑
                                    [Gnome operator: Spark]
```

- Machine has a more vertical emphasis than other machines (tall capture apparatus)
- Prominent cables and input connections visible (representing feeds coming in)
- Output side shows clean, organized source file cards — neat stacks, labeled
- Green glow inside the chamber shows the "capture moment" — raw content being transformed into source material

## Machine-Specific Elements

- Studio microphone on articulating brass arm (the most recognizable element)
- Headphone jack or monitoring headphones draped over a hook
- Small mixing board surface with brass dials and faders
- Cable connections (XLR-style) running into the machine body
- Waveform visualization inside the glass chamber (green glow)
- Output cards labeled or stamped with track indicators
- Small "LIVE" or "REC" indicator light (brass housing, green LED glow)
- Scene plan document pinned to the side or clipped to a small board

## Do / Don't

### Do
- Show the microphone arm — it's the most immediately recognizable element and signals "recording"
- Include visible cables/connections — Produce is about inputs and capture
- Show the green glow with waveform patterns inside the glass — this is the "capture moment"
- Include monitoring equipment (headphones, level meters) — production quality is the point

### Don't
- Show output as finished/polished content — Produce makes raw source material, not final assets
- Use screens or digital displays for the waveform — keep it as a glowing energy pattern inside glass
- Make it look like a radio station or DJ booth — it's a production studio, not entertainment
- Include elements from other stages (packaging, thumbnails, social posts) — those are downstream
