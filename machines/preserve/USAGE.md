# The Memory Vault — Usage Rules

## Visual Identity

**Accent glow color:** `accent-amber` (#FF9800)
**Machine silhouette:** Sturdy, vault-like structure with glass-fronted filing cabinets, card catalog drawers, and a mechanical retrieval arm. The warmest and most furniture-like machine in the factory.
**Signature detail:** Glass-fronted cabinet with amber glow illuminating organized rows of content cards — a visual library. A brass retrieval crane arm that can locate and present any filed item.

## Machine-Specific Elements

- Glass-fronted filing cabinet with warm amber interior glow
- Brass card catalog drawers with small label holders (multiple rows)
- Mechanical retrieval arm/crane that plucks cards from storage
- Output window/display stand where retrieved assets appear
- Leather-bound ledger open on the work surface
- Most furniture-like machine — feels more like a library desk than an industrial press
- Warm, lamp-like amber lighting (not harsh — archival warmth)
- Stacked, organized content cards filed in brass drawer slots
- Small key ring hanging from the vault door (access/organization)

## Do / Don't

### Do
- Emphasize the organized storage — neat rows, labeled drawers, systematic filing
- Include the amber warmth — Preserve is about lasting value, not cold storage
- Show the retrieval mechanism — the point is findability, not just archiving
- Make it feel furniture-like — more library than factory

### Don't
- Make it look like a locked safe or prison — it's a library, not a fortress
- Show disorganized or cluttered storage — Keeper keeps things meticulously organized
- Use cold blue or sterile white lighting — amber warmth is essential to the emotional register
- Include measurement/scoring elements (those belong to Prove)
