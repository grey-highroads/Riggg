# Factory Exterior — Context

## Identity

**Name:** The RIGGG Factory
**Role:** The "hero building" — the primary visual representation of RIGGG as a complete system. Where individual machines represent features, the factory exterior represents the whole platform.

## Business Context

**What it represents:**
The factory exterior is RIGGG's owned media publishing and promotion system visualized as a physical place. It says: "all your content operations happen here, under one roof, managed by a team of specialists."

**When to use it:**
- Homepage hero — the first thing visitors see
- "About the platform" contexts where you need to represent RIGGG holistically
- Pitch decks and overview materials
- Social media profile imagery and banner images

**When NOT to use it:**
- Individual feature explanations (use the specific machine instead)
- Contexts where a single capability is being discussed
- Small-format placements where the detail won't read

## Narrative Role

The factory is the "establishing shot" — it sets the world before you zoom into individual machines and characters. In cinematic terms, it's the wide shot of the city before you cut to a character's apartment. It communicates:

- **Scale:** This is a serious operation, not a one-trick tool
- **System:** The pipes, conveyors, and connections show everything working together
- **Personality:** The whimsical architecture, orange slide, and greenery show this isn't cold enterprise software
- **The conveyor belt with five jars:** This is the product lineup in miniature — each glowing jar is a feature/capability, color-coded to its department

## Key Visual Elements

**The conveyor belt with jars (front center):**
This is the most important narrative device in the image. Five translucent jars on a brass conveyor belt, each containing a glowing icon in a different color. This IS the product offering visualized. Each jar = one RIGGG feature. The icons inside should map to real feature icons once the feature set is confirmed.

**The orange spiral slide:**
Connects upper and lower floors. Suggests fun, energy, and movement within the system. It's also a strong visual differentiator — the warm orange against cool teal creates the composition's most dynamic contrast.

**Glass curtain wall (center):**
Shows the interior — warm, active, busy. The factory isn't just a shell; there's visible work happening inside. This "peek behind the curtain" reinforces transparency and substance.

**Greenery and organic elements:**
Small trees and plants soften the industrial architecture. They signal that RIGGG is a living system, not a cold machine.

**RIGGG signage:**
The wordmark + hexagon logo on the main facade. Clean, modern, positioned as permanent architectural signage — this is the company's home.
