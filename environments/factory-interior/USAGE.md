# Factory Interior — Usage Rules

## Canonical Asset

| Asset | Filename | Use For |
|---|---|---|
| Factory floor wide | `canonical/factory-interior-1.png` | How-it-works sections, process overviews, pipeline illustrations |

## Composition Rules

- **Warm amber lighting dominates** — interior scenes are warmer and more intimate than the exterior
- **Conveyor system visible** — the connecting infrastructure between machines should be clear
- **Multiple machines visible** — at least 2-3 workstations to show the system, but not all 5 need equal detail
- **Pipeline order respected** — if machines are visible left to right, they should follow Produce → Package → Publish → Prove → Preserve
- **Gnomes at stations** — at least one gnome visibly working; others can be background figures

## Prompt Fragment (for generating interior scenes)

```
Interior of the RIGGG factory — a warm, well-lit workshop space with high ceilings and exposed teal pipe networks overhead. Multiple workstations visible along a central brass conveyor belt system connecting them. Warm amber lighting from overhead industrial pendant lamps creates pools of golden light. Teal and bronze machinery lining both sides of the conveyor. Wooden floor with worn patina showing years of use. Small details: tool boards mounted on teal walls, potted plants on shelves between stations, stacked output trays, brass gauge clusters on pipe junctions. The space feels like a master craftsman's workshop — organized, purposeful, and lived-in.
```

## Do / Don't

### Do
- Show the conveyor connections between machines — the system is the point
- Use warm amber lighting — this is the key visual differentiator from the exterior
- Include environmental details (tool boards, plants, worn floor) — the interior should feel lived-in
- Show at least one gnome actively working

### Don't
- Use the same cool lighting as the exterior — the interior is warmer
- Show machines floating independently — they connect via the conveyor
- Clutter the composition — the space should feel organized, not chaotic
- Show all 5 machines at equal detail — pick 2-3 as focal, let others recede
- Use the cream background — interior scenes have walls, floor, and ceiling
