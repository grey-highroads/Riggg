# Anti-Patterns — What the RIGGG World Is NOT

These are the most common failure modes when generating RIGGG assets. Each one has been observed in AI-generated outputs that "look close" but break the brand. Knowing what to reject is as important as knowing what to produce.

## Style Drift

| Drift Direction | What Happens | Why It Fails |
|---|---|---|
| **Too sci-fi** | Chrome surfaces, holographic screens, neon glow, circuit board patterns | RIGGG is mechanical and analog, not digital. The machines are press-and-gear, not screen-and-laser. |
| **Too cartoon** | Flat shading, outline strokes, simplified geometry, exaggerated squash-and-stretch | The world has physical plausibility. Materials behave like real materials under real light. |
| **Too realistic** | Photographic textures, real-world scaling, environmental photography backgrounds | The world is stylized. It's a diorama, not a photograph. |
| **Too steampunk** | Victorian ornamentation, excessive gears-for-decoration, sepia/brown dominance, goggles | RIGGG is modern-industrial-whimsical, not retro-Victorian. Teal dominates, not brown. |
| **Too cute** | Chibi proportions, sparkle effects, pastel overload, emoji-like expressions | The gnomes are skilled workers, not mascots. Warm competence, not kawaii. |
| **Too dark** | Heavy shadows, dramatic contrast, moody lighting, desaturated palette | The world is bright and inviting. Even shadows are warm. |

## Character Mistakes

- **Wrong proportions:** Gnomes stretched to human proportions, or compressed to bobblehead ratios. Maintain 3.5 heads tall.
- **Wrong expression:** Silly, confused, stressed, angry, or exaggerated comedy faces. The gnomes are calm, competent, and take pride in their work.
- **Missing uniform elements:** Hat without logo, missing gloves, wrong boot style, no belt. The uniform is specific — check against the character sheet.
- **Generic fantasy gnome:** Garden gnome aesthetics, red pointy hats, fishing rods, mushroom houses. RIGGG gnomes are industrial workers, not woodland creatures.
- **Multiple gnome styles in one image:** Unless explicitly composing a team scene, gnomes in the same image should share the same art style and proportions. Style inconsistency within a single frame is the most visible form of brand drift.

## Machine Mistakes

- **Accent color as surface paint:** The feature's accent color should only appear as a glow inside translucent glass elements, never as the color of the machine's metal body. Machines are teal and bronze. Color comes from within.
- **No output artifacts:** Machines should visibly produce something — cards, cubes, papers, documents. A machine with no output looks decorative, not functional.
- **Silver/chrome hardware:** All metallic hardware is warm bronze/brass. Chrome reads as modern appliance; brass reads as workshop.
- **Missing conveyor connection:** Machines don't float — they connect to the factory system through belts, tracks, or pipe networks.
- **Overly complex composition:** Each machine should have a clear silhouette. If you can't describe the machine's shape in one sentence, it's too busy.

## Environment Mistakes

- **Colored or gradient backgrounds:** The cream (#FAF5EF) background is sacred. No sky gradients, no color washes, no atmospheric haze.
- **Environmental context bleeding in:** No floor tiles, no wall textures, no horizon lines. The object sits on its own base, surrounded by cream.
- **Scale inconsistency:** A gnome next to a machine that's either too large (building-scale) or too small (desktop-scale) breaks the world. Machines are workstation-scale — operable by a standing gnome.

## Copy Mistakes

- **Generic SaaS language:** "Empower your team," "unlock potential," "revolutionize your workflow." The RIGGG voice is specific about what each capability does.
- **Breaking the metaphor:** The factory metaphor should feel natural, not forced. Don't say "our gnomes will process your content" on a sales page. The metaphor lives in the visuals; the copy can be direct about the product.
- **Overclaiming:** The gnomes are diligent workers, not wizards. The tone is "this works well and we built it carefully" not "this will transform everything."
